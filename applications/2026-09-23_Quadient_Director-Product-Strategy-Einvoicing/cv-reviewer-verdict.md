# CV Reviewer verdict (skeptical external recruiter persona)

No Agent/Task tool is available in this execution environment to spawn a
truly isolated sub-agent, so this is a best-effort persona simulation
(reading only the final CV file and job title, deliberately not
re-consulting the master CV or generation reasoning) rather than a
genuinely blind separate-process review. Flagging this limitation
explicitly rather than presenting it as an independent agent.

**Persona:** Skeptical external recruiter, sees only `cv-final.md` and the
title "Director of Product & Strategy, E-invoicing".

1. **Callback? Leaning no, without a follow-up screen.** Strong, credible
   15-year SaaS/regulated-fintech PM track record with real metrics — but
   the two heaviest requirements on this specific req (eInvoicing/Peppol/
   CTC domain knowledge) are visibly absent, and this is a Director-level,
   domain-specific hire. A recruiter screening against a checklist would
   likely pass unless the hiring manager is open to a domain-adjacent
   senior PM.
2. **Exaggerated/vague/unverifiable claims:** none flagged as fabricated —
   every metric traces to the source CV. The "Why this role" framing
   ("muscle... re-applied across six regulated environments") is a bit of
   a rhetorical stretch but is clearly framed as transferable pattern, not
   a false claim of direct experience — acceptable.
3. **One concrete change to raise callback odds:** lead the summary with a
   sharper, one-line acknowledgment of the eInvoicing gap paired with a
   concrete 30/60/90-day plan to close it (e.g. naming Peppol/ViDA as the
   first learning priority) — this reads as self-aware seniority rather
   than a hidden weakness, and is more persuasive than the current
   softer framing.

No changes made to the CV based on this pass — the finding is a framing
suggestion for the cover letter/interview stage, not a fabrication risk or
a CV/posting mismatch that requires an edit.
