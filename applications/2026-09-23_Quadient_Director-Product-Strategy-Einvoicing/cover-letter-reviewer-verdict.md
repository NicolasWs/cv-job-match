# Cover Letter Reviewer verdict (hiring-manager persona)

Same environment limitation as the CV review: no isolated subagent
available, so this is a disciplined persona read of `outreach-v1.md`'s
cover letter only, against the job posting — not a separate process.

**Persona:** Hiring manager at Quadient for this eInvoicing role, seeing
only the cover letter and posting.

1. **Written specifically for us, or could go anywhere?** Specific — the
   opening paragraph names Peppol, country-specific compliance models, and
   the regulatory-timeline pressure directly from our posting, and the
   closing question about "sequencing the Peppol/ViDA roadmap across
   mandate markets" is not something a generic letter would include. The
   sentence that proves it: "regulatory frameworks that move on government
   timelines... won't slow down for anyone's roadmap."
2. **Cliché/filler/unbacked claims:** none major. No "passionate" or
   "dynamic." The HSBC/Finastra claims are backed by specifics (client
   name, scale). One soft spot: "I'd expect to close it quickly" is an
   assertion without evidence — acceptable as a confidence statement but
   borderline unbacked.
3. **Read-aloud test:** reads like a real, direct person, not
   template-filled — the gap admission in paragraph 2 is unusual for a
   cover letter and reads as candor rather than corporate copy.

**Verdict: strong as-is.** No rewrite triggered — nothing flagged rises to
fabrication or a real CV/posting mismatch. The one soft spot (point 2) is
noted but left as a legitimate confidence statement per guardrails (no
number/metric was invented to prop it up).
