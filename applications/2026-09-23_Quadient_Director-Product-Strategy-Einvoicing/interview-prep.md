## Project Storytelling (STAR) — 3 stories, picked for this posting

### 1. Opensee / HSBC AM — compliance-to-go-live (strongest fit for the eInvoicing "regulation → roadmap" ask)
- **Situation:** Opensee's first SaaS deployment needed to meet HSBC Global
  Asset Management's ISO 27001/SOC 2 security and data requirements before
  go-live — a first for both Opensee and this client segment.
- **Task:** Own the compliance-to-product translation and get the
  deployment live without missing HSBC's internal audit gates.
- **Action:** Worked directly with DevOps to scope and sequence the
  compliance workstream, prioritizing the items that blocked go-live over
  nice-to-have hardening, and kept HSBC's audit stakeholders aligned
  throughout.
- **Result:** Technical go-live achieved end of 2023; delivered 99%+
  uptime against a 95% contractual SLA over the following six months, with
  direct CTO-level reporting.
- **Learning:** Compliance requirements are a product input, not a
  blocker, when someone owns translating them into a sequenced roadmap —
  directly the muscle this Quadient role needs for Peppol/CTC mandates.

### 2. Finastra — regulatory advisory at scale across a matrixed global org
- **Situation:** 30+ large financial institutions needed their risk/market
  platforms (Sophis, FusionRisk) aligned to evolving regulatory
  requirements, across multiple regions and languages.
- **Task:** Lead discovery, solution design, and workshop delivery to keep
  clients compliant and engaged commercially.
- **Action:** Ran in-person workshops across five countries, coordinated a
  12-person presales team for consistent value messaging, and led RFP
  responses on regulated deals from $500K to $3M.
- **Result:** 65% increase in sales-qualified opportunities from this
  advisory approach.
- **Learning:** Regulatory complexity, handled well, is a commercial
  accelerant — directly transferable to positioning Quadient's eInvoicing
  suite in competitive, regulation-driven deals.

### 3. Freelance AI-SDLC build — fast, technical delivery under a hard deadline
- **Situation:** A French asset manager needed a broker-voting web app
  built and live within a tight window.
- **Task:** Deliver the full product, from design to production go-live,
  as an AI-assisted, largely solo build.
- **Action:** Used a full AI-assisted SDLC (Angular/.NET/SQL Server) to
  compress the traditional build timeline.
- **Result:** Design to production go-live in under two months.
- **Learning:** Proof of current, hands-on technical fluency and speed —
  relevant to Quadient's stated push toward a cloud-native, AI-enabled
  eInvoicing platform.
