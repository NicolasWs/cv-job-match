**Cover Letter Reviewer** (hiring-manager persona)
- Verdict: "Yes, largely" feels written specifically for UpSlide — cited the QA-loop/golden-dataset paragraph and the Refine-specific ask as proof. Flagged one real gap (no explicit cross-functional Eng/Design mention) and a soft, generic CTA.
