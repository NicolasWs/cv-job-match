## About the role
Bridge technical execution and business value for a defined portfolio of
pre-identified product features, under direct guidance of the Senior
Manager of Product Management, across two areas:
- Persona-Specific Features (Visual Graph, Visual Edit, Visual GLM) — new
  user personas via targeted use cases.
- Dataiku Business Applications — packaged, task-oriented, Dataiku-managed
  apps with consumer-grade non-technical front ends over Dataiku's platform.

## Key Responsibilities
- Market & Customer Feedback Coordination: market vision, targeted market
  reviews/fact-finding, run customer meetings to collect/qualify feedback,
  outreach coordination.
- Guided Product Scoping & Execution: own roadmap centralization/priorities
  for the scope, build consensus with stakeholders incl. SVP Product & VP
  Engineering; write clear user stories/functional requirements for
  pre-identified, simple features; UX collaboration on wireframes/flows;
  support engineering/TPM during development; coordinate/execute basic test
  plans against acceptance criteria.
- Launch Support & Tracking: own pre-launch materials (docs, release notes,
  internal demos); implement/track pre-defined KPIs post-launch.

## Qualifications
- 3–5 years in Product Management or Product Owner in agile software dev.
- Proven foundational PM lifecycle knowledge: clear user stories,
  acceptance criteria, tactical backlog management from day one.
- Experience with data tools, analytics platforms, or data science use
  cases; solid understanding of data workflows/data science concepts.
  Direct experience/familiarity with LLMs/GenAI, AI Agents, or
  plugin/extension ecosystems = strong plus.
- Strong organizational/communication skills, collaborative, adapts to
  international cross-cultural teams.
- Growth mindset — eager to dive into new technical/business domains.

Location: Paris. Seniority: Cadre (non-executive professional). Full-time.
Function: Product Management & Marketing. Industry: Software Development.
