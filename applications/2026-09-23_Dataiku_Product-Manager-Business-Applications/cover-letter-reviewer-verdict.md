# Cover Letter Reviewer Verdict — Dataiku, Product Manager – Business Applications

**Note on method:** No Agent/subagent tool was available in this session.
This verdict was produced as a persona-constrained review pass — reading
ONLY outreach-v2.md's cover letter and the job posting text, not the CV or
prior drafts — rather than a true isolated subagent. Flagged for
transparency.

**Persona:** hiring manager at Dataiku, Product Management org, seeing
only the final cover letter and the job posting.

1. **Written specifically for us, or could go to anyone?** Specifically
   for Dataiku. The proof sentence: "you're solving the exact problem I
   solved this year — turning a complex technical back end into a
   consumer-grade experience a business expert can actually use" —
   directly mirrors the posting's own "consumer-grade, non-technical front
   ends" language for Business Applications, and names Visual Graph/Visual
   Edit by their actual posting names. This could not be sent to another
   company unedited.

2. **Cliché / filler / unbacked claims:** none of the classic clichés
   ("passionate," "dynamic," "team player") appear. Every claim ties to a
   specific artifact (the broker-voting app, the Opensee uptime figure,
   the Dataiku certifications). No unbacked numbers.

3. **Read-aloud test:** reads like a real person explaining why they
   specifically want this job, not a template. The one place it slightly
   over-reaches rhetorically is "not because a job posting asked, but
   because I wanted to understand the platform I'd want to build on" —
   true per the CV timeline (certifications predate this specific posting
   surfacing), but worth Nicolas confirming mentally before sending, since
   it's a claim about motivation rather than a fact, and should only be
   said if it's genuinely how he'd describe his own reasoning.

**Verdict: Yes, largely.** No fabrication or narrative-principle conflict
found. No CV/cover-letter edit made as a result of this review beyond the
salutation tweak already applied in v2 during the self-review pass.
