# CV Reviewer Verdict — Dataiku, Product Manager – Business Applications

**Note on method:** No Agent/subagent tool was available in this session to
spawn an isolated reviewer process. This verdict was produced as a
persona-constrained review pass — reading ONLY cv-v2.md and the job title,
deliberately not referencing the analysis.md edit plan or prior reasoning
— rather than a true blind subagent. Flagged here for transparency; it is
a real independent pass, not a rubber stamp, but it is a weaker guarantee
of blindness than a separate subagent.

**Persona:** skeptical external recruiter, sees only cv-v2.md + the job
title "Product Manager – Business Applications."

1. **Callback? Yes.** The Dataiku-specific certifications sitting at the
   top of the CV are the single strongest signal a recruiter screening this
   role could see — they answer "does this person already know our
   platform" before the recruiter even reads the experience section. The
   broker-voting app story maps almost one-to-one onto "packaged,
   task-oriented app for Business Experts."

2. **Exaggerated/vague/unverifiable claims:** none flagged as fabricated.
   One soft spot: "comfortable translating data-science and platform
   concepts for both business experts and engineering teams" in the
   summary is a claim without its own dedicated proof point elsewhere in
   the CV — it's supported indirectly (Opensee onboarding workshops,
   HSBC training) but never stated plainly as a bridging skill in the
   experience bullets themselves.

3. **One concrete change to most raise callback odds:** the years-of-
   experience mismatch (15+ years total vs. the posting's 3–5y band) is
   not addressed anywhere in the CV itself — only in interview-prep.md.
   A recruiter doing a fast scan may screen this out purely on inferred
   seniority/comp expectations before ever reaching the mitigating
   context. Consider one explicit, honest line in the summary naming the
   role level sought (e.g., "seeking an individual-contributor PM role
   with guided scope") to pre-empt that screen-out — this is a real risk
   worth surfacing to Nicolas, not a wording nitpick.

**Action taken:** Flagged risk is real but addressing it requires a
judgment call from Nicolas (whether to self-disclose seniority framing in
the CV vs. handle it live in conversation/interview). Not changed in the
CV body per this review alone — recorded here and in interview-prep.md's
"Weaknesses" section as the mitigation path; no unilateral CV edit made
because the two options (add a line vs. rely on interview framing) are a
strategic choice, not a factual gap or narrative-principle conflict.
