## Project Storytelling (STAR)

### 1. Opensee — HSBC AM production SLA
- **Situation:** Opensee's first Cloud, first asset-manager client (HSBC
  AM) — high stakes for the platform's credibility.
- **Task:** Own onboarding, define adoption metrics, and hit a
  contractual uptime SLA.
- **Action:** Defined reporting-automation and data-transformation
  adoption metrics; ran the onboarding and managed-services relationship.
- **Result:** Guaranteed 95% uptime, delivered 99%+ for the first six
  months including managed data services.
- **Learning:** A first enterprise client is a design partner as much as
  a customer — the metrics you pick together become the product's
  north star.

### 2. Finastra — 30+ institution discovery & RFPs
- **Situation:** Sophis/FusionRisk platform capabilities needed to keep
  pace with evolving regulatory and technical requirements across a large
  client base.
- **Task:** Lead discovery, solution design, and demo delivery; support
  competitive RFPs.
- **Action:** Ran in-person workshops across five cities, coordinated RFP
  responses, supervised 12 presales consultants for consistency.
- **Result:** +65% sales-qualified opportunities; supported $500K-$3M
  upsell/renewal deals.
- **Learning:** Enterprise buying is a team sport — consistent value
  messaging across a large presales team matters as much as the pitch
  itself.

### 3. Freelance — AI-assisted platform build, design to production
- **Situation:** French asset manager needed a broker-voting web app,
  fast.
- **Task:** Deliver end-to-end, from design to production go-live.
- **Action:** Used an AI-assisted software development lifecycle to
  compress the build; owned the full delivery loop myself.
- **Result:** Design-to-production in under two months.
- **Learning:** AI-assisted delivery changes what "PM who stays close to
  execution" can mean in practice — I now have first-hand proof of that,
  not just an opinion about it.
