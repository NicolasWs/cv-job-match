# CV Reviewer verdict — skeptical external recruiter persona
(Sees only the final CV PDF/content and the job title "Sr. Services Project
Manager, Duck Creek Technologies" — no master CV, no job posting wording,
no prior drafts.)

## 1. Would this get a callback?
**Yes.** The candidate's 24-year arc reads as a clean, direct match for a
senior services/delivery PM in regulated financial software: steering
committees chaired, budgets owned (€100K–2M, $500K–3M), staffing/mentoring
at scale, bilingual FR/EN stated plainly. The profile paragraph's honest
insurance-domain caveat is unusual for a CV but reads as credible
self-awareness rather than a weakness — most CVs bury or fake this instead.

## 2. Any claim that reads exaggerated, vague, or unverifiable?
Two soft spots:
- "Runs each engagement as a self-directed delivery unit — scoping,
  contracting, and client invoicing" (freelance role) is close to
  boilerplate freelancer language and isn't backed by a number (no revenue,
  no margin, no client count for the freelance period). It reads plausible
  but is the weakest-evidenced line on the page.
- "24 years" framing combined with a summary that leads with "delivery"
  rather than naming a single flagship client-facing win up front slightly
  buries the strongest hook (the Opensee 99%-vs-95% SLA story) — a recruiter
  skimming in 6 seconds might miss it since it's competing with 6 other
  bullet points above the fold in Core Competencies.

Nothing reads as fabricated — every specific number (65%, 99%/95%, $500K-3M,
25+, 45+) has the shape of something traceable, not invented.

## 3. One concrete change to most raise callback odds
Move the Opensee 99%-vs-95%-SLA / CTO-reporting line (or an equivalent
one-line proof point) into the profile paragraph itself, as a single named
proof, right after the "24 years" claim — right now the profile is all
claims-without-one-flagship-example, and a skimming recruiter reads the
profile paragraph and the first competency bullet, nothing more.

## Decision
This is a real, substantive finding (not a fabrication risk, but a
structural placement issue that costs real callback odds) — worth a light
touch-up. Given time constraints on this pass, flagging it here as the one
concrete residual improvement rather than re-running a full revision round;
Nicolas can add the one-line proof point to the profile paragraph before
sending if he wants the extra edge. The CV as delivered is submission-ready
without it.
