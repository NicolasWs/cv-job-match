# Cover Letter Reviewer verdict — hiring manager persona
(Sees only the final cover letter and the Duck Creek job posting — not the
CV, not previous drafts, not this conversation.)

## 1. Written specifically for us, or could it go to anyone?
Mostly specific. The opening sentence names Duck Creek's actual
positioning ("intelligent solutions provider... P&C insurance... modern
SaaS platform") and ties it directly to the role's delivery-discipline ask
— that couldn't be pasted into a generic application unchanged. The
proof-point paragraph (Sophis, Opensee, Finastra) is candidate-specific but
company-agnostic — it would read almost identically in a cover letter for
any regulated-industry services PM role. The sentence that proves this
letter is for Duck Creek specifically: "it's a modern SaaS platform
business asking its services organization to run the same discipline...
that I've spent 24 years delivering inside regulated financial
institutions, just in a different vertical."

## 2. Any cliché, filler, or unbacked claim?
No clichés ("passionate," "dynamic," "synergy" are all absent). No filler
sentences. Every number traces to something specific (€100K-2M, $500K-3M,
30+ institutions, 25+ consultants) — nothing reads as an invented KPI. One
soft spot: "I'd like to talk" as a closing CTA is a bit flat/generic —
functional but not memorable.

## 3. Read-aloud test — does it sound like a real person?
Yes. The "I'll be direct about the one gap" paragraph is the strongest
tell of a real person writing this — it's the kind of unguarded, slightly
risky honesty that a template or a nervous candidate wouldn't include. The
rest reads a touch dense/list-like in the middle paragraph (three
companies, three proof points, one long paragraph) — would benefit from a
line break for skimmability, but the content itself sounds authored, not
generated.

## Verdict
Solid, submission-ready. Nothing here rises to a fabrication or narrative-
principle violation that requires a fix. The one honest improvement
(splitting the middle paragraph for skimmability) is cosmetic and left to
Nicolas's judgment before sending — not applied here to avoid an
unnecessary extra revision round on a letter that already plateaued.
