## About the job
Are you ready to shape the future of capital markets? We are looking for a Project Manager to support the launch and coordination of a new initiative within the Primary Markets team.

You will support the Project Lead directly in structuring, coordinating and driving the different project workstreams across business, product, technology, legal, regulatory, operations, sales and finance. You will play a central role in ensuring project governance, planning, execution discipline, stakeholder coordination and consolidated reporting.

Transversal role requiring strong project management discipline, excellent stakeholder management skills, and the ability to operate in an evolving strategic environment.

## Responsibilities

### Project management
- Structure, coordinate and monitor the overall project plan across workstreams (product, technology, legal, regulatory, operations, finance, sales, go-to-market).
- Prepare governance materials: agendas, presentations, decision papers, minutes, action logs, follow-up trackers.
- Monitor scope, timeline, milestones, dependencies, resources, risks and issues.
- Maintain master project plan, decision log, action tracker, status reports.
- Ensure timely follow-up of actions, escalation of blockers, alignment between workstreams.
- Coordinate interactions with internal stakeholders across Euronext geographies and functions.
- Produce consolidated reporting for the Project Lead, senior management, governance bodies.
- Support coordination of external advisers, consultants, technology vendors.
- Ensure compliance with internal processes: GDPR, InfoSec, cyber risk, operational risk, vendor management.

### Product design support
- Support the Project Lead and future Product Owner in structuring the target product vision, solution design and delivery roadmap.
- Contribute to defining/documenting use cases, client journeys, functional requirements, operating model assumptions.
- Coordinate market analysis, competitive benchmarking, client feedback consolidation.
- Support business case inputs: revenue assumptions, cost estimates, investment needs, delivery scenarios.
- Align product design with technology/regulatory/legal/operational/go-to-market constraints.
- Track product design decisions, open questions, dependencies, required arbitrations.
- Support translation of business/user needs into structured requirements for technology teams.
