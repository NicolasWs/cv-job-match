## Honest note on scoring methodology
Automated keyword scoring initially over-credited several jobs (e.g. a
"Test Lead IA" role at AXA, "Head of Partner Marketing" at Mistral AI, and
"Senior Commercial Legal Counsel" at Mistral AI all scored 9/10 on pure
keyword overlap despite not being PM/PO roles at all). A role-function gate
(title must contain a PM/PO/product-leadership keyword) was applied before
finalizing scores, and the top 6 candidates were individually re-reviewed
against the actual job description text — this dropped Photoroom, Datadog,
and one Qonto posting (German-language, real skill gap) out of the 9/10
band. Only LSEG and Qonto (Staff PM, AI Products) survive at genuine 9/10 —
both already have existing application packages, so nothing new crosses the
9.3 auto-prep bar this scan.
